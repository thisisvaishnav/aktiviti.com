Frequently asked questions

Something else? Email support@aktiviti.app and a person replies.

Do I pay anything up front?

No. The codes cost nothing until a buyer redeems one. At the end of the month you get the list of codes redeemed and an invoice for them at your partner price, and that is all you ever pay.

What is the partner price?

A discount on our price, agreed with you before the first codes go out. Write and tell us how you sell and how many codes you expect to need, and we agree it with you.

What price should I charge?

Your own. Most partners stay close to our price in their currency, so a buyer who compares does not feel caught out, but the price is yours to set.

When is an invoice due?

Within 30 days. By then every code on it is past the 14-day refund window, so the amount is final. If an invoice goes unpaid, your unused codes are paused until it is paid. Codes your buyers have already redeemed keep working.

What if a buyer wants a refund?

Refund them yourself, the way you refund anything else you sell, and send us the code within 14 days of it being redeemed. We switch it off and take it off your invoice. A redeemed code that is switched off takes its Mac back from the license.

Who handles sales tax and VAT?

You do. You are the seller to your buyers, so sales tax, VAT and receipts are yours to handle.

Who can I sell codes to?

To the people who will use them, not to other resellers or key sites. Codes that turn up for sale somewhere else are switched off.

Can I use the Aktiviti name and screenshots?

Yes, as they are. Describe Aktiviti truthfully, and use our name, icon and screenshots without changing them. The facts above are ready to copy.

Can I sell Aktiviti in a bundle?

Yes. Tell us what else is in it and the price. The codes work the same way, and the invoice counts the ones redeemed.

How do I get codes for a giveaway?

Ask. For a review or a giveaway we send a handful of free codes, and the buyer redeems them the same way, on aktivitimac.com/redeem.

What happens if either of us stops?

Either of us can stop at any time. Codes your buyers have already redeemed keep working, and the unused ones are switched off.

Who answers my email?

Atta, who builds Aktiviti. Every request is read by a person and answered within a day or two.
