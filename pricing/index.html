Questions about buying

How much does it cost?

Two ways to buy, both one payment. Lifetime, with every update for life, is $39 for one Mac at the regular price. The launch price started at $5 for the first 1,000 licenses and rises in steps as they sell, up to the regular $39. The pricing card shows the price today, how many are left at it, and what it goes to next. The count comes from our licensing server, so it is the real number. A year of updates is $9 for one Mac. Aktiviti with our two other apps, Flit and Levers, is $49 for all three.

Do I pay again for updates?

With Lifetime, no: every update is included for life, with no paid upgrades and nothing to renew. With a year of updates, you get every version released in the next twelve months, and the app keeps working after that for as long as you like. Only a version released after your year needs a renewal, and renewing is your choice.

What happens when the year of updates ends?

Nothing, unless you want a newer version. The license never expires: the version you have keeps working, and the app stops offering versions released after your year. If you install one by hand, it tells you the year has ended and points you back to the last version your license includes. Renew whenever you want to move on.

Do prices include tax?

No. Prices are before tax. Where your country charges VAT, GST or sales tax, Paddle adds it at checkout and passes it on to your tax office.

What are Flit and Levers?

Our two other Mac apps. Flit is dictation that never leaves your Mac: speak, and the words appear wherever you type. Levers hides the desktop, the Dock and menu bar icons before you record your screen, and puts them back in one click. Fore Plus is $49 for Aktiviti, Flit and Levers on one key. Fore Insider is $99 for those and every app we release later. Each covers one Mac for each app, with every update for life.

What is coming next?

The roadmap at aktivitimac.com/roadmap lists it in order: a storage map of what takes your disk space; safe cleanup of caches and old build files; the health of your drives; disk activity for each connected drive; and Mac Health, one line on how your Mac is doing. It also says what comes later and what was asked for and won’t be built. There are no dates. A lifetime license includes all of it, and a year of updates includes what ships during the year.

How many Macs can I use it on?

As many as you pay for. One license covers one Mac. For more, pick a pack of 2, 5, 10 or 25 Macs on the pricing card. You get one license key that works on that many Macs, and each Mac costs less as the pack gets bigger: $25 for 2, down to about $8 a Mac in the pack of 25. Move a license to a new Mac whenever you like, with no account and no email to support.

How does the 50% back offer work?

Buy Aktiviti, post about it on X, Threads, Reels, TikTok or Shorts, and email us the link. At 5,000 views we refund half of what you paid, and at 20,000 views we refund all of it. On X and Threads it takes 10,000 and 50,000 views. You keep your license. The cash back page has the rules.

Is there a student or teacher discount?

Yes, half price, for students, teachers and school staff. Email support@aktiviti.app from your school email address and we reply with a one-use code for one Mac. The details are at aktivitimac.com/education.

Is there a free trial?

Yes, 3 days with every feature, and no account or card. Download Aktiviti and choose Try Free when it opens. After you buy, there is still a 14-day refund.

How do I get the app after paying?

The download link is on the page you land on after checkout. Your license key arrives by email within a minute. If you lose either, enter your key on the download page to get the link again.

Why is it not on the Mac App Store?

The App Store sandbox stops an app from reading other apps' processes, which is the one thing an activity monitor has to do. Aktiviti is signed with our Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app.
